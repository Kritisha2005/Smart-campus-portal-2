General Placement Preparation Process

01. Registration

Stay informed about placement activities and complete the required registration process.

02. Skill Preparation

Work on technical knowledge, aptitude, communication and interview skills.

03. Application

Review available opportunities and follow the instructions shared by the placement cell.

04. Selection Activities

Participate in the selection stages specified by the recruiting organization.

05. Further Guidance

Follow official communication for updates about the selection process and next steps.

Note: This is a general preparation overview. Actual placement procedures, eligibility and schedules depend on official instructions.
