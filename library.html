📖 About the Central Library

The Central Library of Kanpur Institute of Technology supports students and faculty in academic learning, research and knowledge development.

A library is an important part of every educational institution. It provides access to books, reference materials and a peaceful environment where students can develop their knowledge beyond classroom learning.

Students can use library resources for semester preparation, assignments, technical projects, presentations and independent study.

Our Purpose: To encourage reading habits, independent learning and continuous academic improvement.

✨ Library Facilities

The library supports different academic and self-learning needs through its learning resources.

📘 Textbooks

Textbooks help students understand their subjects and prepare for classroom learning and semester examinations.

📚 Reference Books

Reference materials can support deeper understanding of topics and academic project preparation.

📰 Journals & Magazines

Academic journals and magazines help readers explore educational topics and technical developments.

🪑 Reading Space

A quiet study environment helps students concentrate on reading, revision and independent learning.

💻 Digital Resources

Digital learning resources may be available to support online academic research and subject learning.

🗞️ Newspapers

Newspapers and general reading materials can help students stay informed about current affairs.

📚 Available Learning Resources

Students can explore learning materials related to different academic and general knowledge areas.

  • Engineering and Technology: Subject-related books and technical concepts.
  • Computer Science and IT: Programming, software, databases and computing.
  • Management: Business studies, management and communication.
  • Science and Mathematics: Fundamental concepts and academic reference material.
  • General Knowledge: General reading and awareness-related material.

📋 Library Rules and Guidelines

Every student is expected to follow library discipline so that everyone can use the learning environment comfortably.

  1. Maintain silence inside the library.
  2. Carry your valid college ID card.
  3. Keep books and other reading materials in good condition.
  4. Return issued books by their due date.
  5. Do not write, highlight or damage library books.
  6. Keep the reading area clean and organised.
  7. Follow instructions given by library staff.
  8. Use library resources responsibly.
  9. Respect other students who are studying.
Remember: Maintaining discipline helps create a better learning environment for all students.

🎓 Book Issue and Return Policy

Students can borrow books for academic learning according to the library's issue and return policy.

📖 Book Issue

  • Students can issue books for one month.
  • Keep track of the book's due date.
  • Handle borrowed books carefully.
  • Check the book condition before borrowing.

🔄 Book Return and Reissue

  • Return books on or before the due date.
  • Students can request reissue of books.
  • Reissue is subject to library approval and applicable rules.
  • Contact library staff if you need help with a return.

💰 Late Return Fine

A fine of ₹5 per day is charged for each day a book is overdue without return or reissue.

💡 Tips for Effective Library Study

Make your library visits more productive by following a few simple study habits.

  • Set a study goal: Decide which subject or topic you want to study.
  • Choose useful resources: Use textbooks and reference books related to your topic.
  • Prepare notes: Write down important concepts in your own words.
  • Revise regularly: Review previously studied topics to improve understanding.
  • Use your time wisely: Focus on your learning goals during your visit.

Students who have questions about book availability, issue procedures, return dates, reissue or library rules should contact the library staff.

  • Ask about applicable reissue procedures.
